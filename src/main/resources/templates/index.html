About the Portal

The Service Request Management System provides a centralized interface for employees to submit and monitor IT service requests.

The portal organizes request information such as the issue, description, priority, status, assignment, and resolution details so that requests can be tracked throughout their lifecycle.

The system is designed to provide a simple and structured way for employees to communicate IT service requirements and monitor request progress.
